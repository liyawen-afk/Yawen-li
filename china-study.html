Study Abroad to China

China's Healthcare Revolution: Tradition Meets Innovation

Program Leaders: Dr. Jie Yu (Nursing) & Dr. Yawen Li (Social Work)

Travel Dates: Offered every Spring Break.

Students from California State University, San Bernardino recently returned from an immersive study abroad experience in China, where they explored health and healthcare systems in Beijing and Shanghai. The program, co-taught by Dr. Jie Yu and Dr. Yawen Li, brought together 19 students from a wide range of disciplines, including social work, nursing, sociology, psychology, child development, computer science, natural sciences and Spanish.

Centered on the theme of health and healthcare in China, the program combined academic learning with experiential engagement. Students visited leading healthcare institutions such as Guanganmen Hospital, as well as Boai Chinese Medicine Clinic. These visits provided hands-on exposure to practices such as cupping, acupuncture and moxibustion, allowing students to directly experience approaches to care that emphasize balance and holistic well-being.

Student Reflection

“This trip changed my perspective on both the world and my career. In the U.S., care can often feel less centered on the individual... In China, I saw a more holistic perspective—one that emphasizes overall well-being and recognizes how factors like diet, family, culture and lifestyle are interconnected.”
