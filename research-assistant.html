Student Research Assistant

Adult Day Health Centers Study — Dynamic Capabilities Project

Principal Investigator: Yawen Li, PhD, MSW
School of Social Work, California State University, San Bernardino

We are recruiting Student Research Assistants for an NIH-funded study examining the dynamic capabilities of Adult Day Health Centers across the United States. This is an opportunity to engage in meaningful, nationally funded research on a critical and understudied component of community-based care for older adults.

Why This Opportunity Matters

Highly relevant for students planning to apply to graduate or PhD programs.
Research experience is among the most important factors in graduate and doctoral admissions. This position places you on a named NIH-funded project, exposes you to the full research cycle, and connects you with a faculty mentor. If you are considering a PhD, master's program, or research career, this role provides the documented experience that sets competitive applicants apart.

Responsibilities

  • Conduct systematic literature reviews
  • Assist in developing research protocols
  • Organize and conduct qualitative interviews
  • Data collection and data entry
  • Support qualitative/quantitative data analysis
  • Attend research team meetings
  • Assist with dissemination of findings

Requirements

  • Undergraduate or graduate students in any relevant field
  • Qualitative and/or quantitative research interest (all levels welcome)
  • Strong organizational skills and attention to detail
  • Clear written and verbal communication abilities
  • Interest in aging, healthcare, or community health research

Position Details

Hours per Week Up to 20 hrs
Hourly Rate $18 – $20 / hr

Please submit your application through the link below. We welcome students at all experience levels who bring curiosity, rigor, and a commitment to community health research.
